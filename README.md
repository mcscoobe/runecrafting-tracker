# Runecrafting Tracker

Track the runes you craft and your total GP profit per session.

[Plugin Hub listing](https://github.com/runelite/plugin-hub/blob/master/plugins/runecrafting-tracker)

## Features

- Tracks all 22 craftable rune types, including combination and Aether runes
- Tracks runes crafted into your inventory and into your rune pouch
- Shows crafted count and GP profit per rune, plus a running total
- Session data is saved and restored automatically — counts persist across restarts
- Works at all altars, Ourania (ZMI), Guardians of the Rift, and Zeah altars
- Right-click the profit header to reset all counts
